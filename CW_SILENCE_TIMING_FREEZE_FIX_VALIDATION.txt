MadModem 0.5.78 — CW silence timing freeze fix
Validation date: 2026-08-04

LIVE FAILURE ADDRESSED
----------------------
- Real station initially near 17-22 WPM.
- During a long pause, stale carrier evidence previously admitted noise runs.
- Previous visible result: 48-50 WPM, older paths up to 70 WPM, and E/T-heavy text.
- Corrected design: timing feed freezes after an adaptive >7-dit silence and
  preserves the trusted WPM/dit/dah prior.

CORE CORRECTIONS
----------------
- Separate permissive lane-maintenance and strict timing-centred PSD gates.
- Strict acquisition requires repeated carrier evidence and a unique selected peak.
- Carrier evidence attack/release changed from +1/-1 to +2/-4.
- Fixed 0.9 s hold replaced by adaptive 8.5-dit hold.
- Fixed 0.8 s loss delay replaced by adaptive 1.8-dit confirmation.
- Timing reset retains trusted pair evidence as well as the timing means.
- Relative-pair gap constrained to 0.32-2.10 times the short MARK.
- Established pair/threshold consistency tightened.
- Dit/dah adaptation is pair-only; isolated MARKs cannot change WPM.
- Cluster step limited to 1.5 percent per accepted pair.
- Long/short timing ratio capped at 4.2.
- Public WPM changes only while a qualified carrier clock is observable.
- Auto-WPM range remains 5-50 WPM.
