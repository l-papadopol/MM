MadModem 0.5.79 — FT live topology restore
================================================

Baseline
--------
MadModem_0_5_79_RTTY_CONTEST_MACRO_UI_FIX_FULL_SOURCE

Regression evidence
-------------------
- The last source proven to decode FT8 efficiently on the target Windows PC
  was MadModem_0_5_78_FT_LINEAR_SEQUENCER_FIX_FULL_SOURCE.
- Its live resource topology on the 6C/12T target was:
    FT pool=12, reserved=2, live gate/boundary/OSD=4/10/3
- The later, not-yet-radio-validated FT_DECODE_DEADLINE_FIX changed that to:
    FT pool=12, reserved=0, live gate/boundary/OSD=12/12/4
- The 12/12/4 policy remained byte-identical in 0.5.79 and in the RTTY
  contest/macro/UI source used by the failing live sessions.
- In the failing FT8 log, the gate repeatedly reached 260 candidates but
  attempted LDPC only 0--4 times and decoded nothing.  The boundary similarly
  admitted roughly 320--340 candidates but attempted LDPC only 8--15 times.
- In the proven source, normal gate batches decoded about 5--13 signals and
  attempted LDPC about 19--39 times on comparable live sessions.

Root mechanism
--------------
The FT8 candidate finder partitions its pre-candidate matrix by worker count
and applies a per-partition top-K limit before the global merge.  Worker count
therefore affects candidate admission, not only elapsed time.  Changing the
gate from four to twelve workers changes the candidate set reaching Costas
refinement and the pre-LDPC hard-sync gate.

Correction
----------
- Restored utils/SystemResourceManager.cpp byte-for-byte from the proven
  FT_LINEAR_SEQUENCER_FIX source.
- On the 6C/12T target this restores reserved=2 and gate/boundary/OSD=4/10/3.
- Runtime resource observations remain telemetry-only and cannot ratchet the
  topology down during a capture.
- Offline work may still use the complete persistent pool.

- FT8 and FT4 candidate, Costas, LDPC, CRC, OSD and subtraction code.
- AudioEngine, resampling, timestamping and live slot assembly.
- FT sequencer, TX/RX ownership, CAT/PTT and waterfall.
- RTTY live decoder, contest rules, contest UI, macros and logging.
- FT caller queue and session UI.
- Build flags and MadModem application version 0.5.79.
