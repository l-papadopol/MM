MadModem 0.5.79 - RTTY contest side-panel layout correction
================================================================

Observed defect
---------------
The dedicated RTTY contest page had a horizontal minimum size substantially
larger than the 300-340 px side panel. Two layouts caused the overflow:

- the contest header used label + editor + action button in three columns;
- the QSO editor used two label/editor pairs in four columns.

The QScrollArea therefore exposed a horizontal scrollbar. Depending on its
position, either the left labels/group titles or the right editor/action column
was clipped. Expanding buttons then filled the oversized content width and
showed excessive empty space around their captions.

Correction
----------

- The page width is owned by the scroll viewport; horizontal scrolling is off.
- The contest header is a two-column label/editor form.
- Reload and new-session actions are text-sized, right-aligned controls on
  independent rows, so translations cannot recreate a wide third column.
- The QSO editor is a vertical two-column label/editor form.
- QSO fields may shrink horizontally while retaining the full side-panel width.
- Add-QSO is text-sized and right aligned.
- The six macro buttons use three compact columns and have no forced minimum
  width, eliminating the large unused space around short captions.
- Page/group/button padding is reduced locally, without changing the global
  cockpit theme.
- The outer tab caption is shortened to "Contest"; the internal group title
  remains the localized "Contest mode" / "Modalita contest".
- Only vertical scrolling remains when page height exceeds the window.

Non-regression scope
--------------------
No RTTY decoder, CAT, contest rules, macros, scoring, QSO mirroring, logging,
audio, waterfall, FT8/FT4, or sequencer behavior was changed.

Automated audit
---------------
Run:

    python3 scripts/check_rtty_contest_layout.py

The existing RTTY runtime audit remains applicable:

    python3 scripts/check_rtty_live_contest_runtime.py
