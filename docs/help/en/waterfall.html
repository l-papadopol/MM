Waterfall and markers

The waterfall shows the audio passband used by the selected mode. Left-click a signal to move the RX marker; in FT4/FT8, right-click moves the TX marker. Optional FT and RTTY labels can be shown over the spectrum.

Zoom and movement

Use the mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer. Shift+wheel, a horizontal wheel or middle-button drag pans the visible range. Left double-click restores the full passband. Markers and overlays remain tied to their real audio frequencies.

CW and RTTY skimmers

Skimmer text and CQ labels are controlled from the respective Mode panel. Text uses separate vertical columns, CQ calls use labels. Zoom to make room for crowded signals. The RTTY selected-station text trail is replaced while Skimmer is enabled. Neither display starts a transmission or writes a QSO.

Drag the horizontal divider above the waterfall to change its height. Taller views show more characters per skimmer column; the height is remembered. The full 100–3000 Hz scale uses ticks at 100, 400, 700, 1000, 1400, 1800, 2200, 2600 and 3000 Hz. Zoomed views use rounded frequencies at their actual positions.
