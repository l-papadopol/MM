MadModem 0.5.8 R6 — FT8 active-QSO deadline priority
=====================================================

Observed failure
----------------

The 2026-08-18 live log proves that the general FT8 gate itself was not slow:
it normally completed in 390–446 ms. During the SV2HTW QSO, however, the gate
did not admit the selected correspondent. The sequencer therefore started a
retry, and only the complete boundary pass recovered the reply 1.3–1.4 seconds
later. The ZL1RPC QSO worked when the same correspondent happened to survive
the gate pruning.

Implemented policy
------------------

* There remains one Ft8RxDecoder job and one worker pool; no concurrent or
  fallback decoder was added.
* During an active FT8 QSO gate only, findCandidates reserves at most 32
  candidates within +/-90 Hz of the tracked RX marker.
* That bounded quota has its own 0.92 sync floor and does not consume the
  existing normal wideband candidate quota.
* The reserved candidates enter LDPC before wideband candidates. Controlled
  metric recovery is allowed for this small set, with GF(2) OSD capped at four
  attempts and 20 ms of cumulative worker budget.
* Early completion requires the decoded message to contain both the configured
  DX callsign and, when configured, the local callsign.
* Once the target is recovered, the gate returns it immediately. Non-QSO
  wideband LDPC is left to the later boundary. If the target is not recovered,
  the established wideband gate runs with its original candidate count.

Expected live evidence
----------------------

For a successful priority decode the runtime profiler reports:

  FT8 QSO deadline: <DXCALL> decoded first at <Hz> Hz; wideband gate deferred

This line must occur before the following TX boundary. On-air validation should
also confirm that the sequencer arms the next message from that gate result,
without starting an obsolete retry first.

Automated guards
----------------

  python3 scripts/check_ft_qso_deadline_priority.py
  bash scripts/check_ft_wideband_sensitivity.sh
  python3 scripts/check_ft_linear_sequencer.py

The new guard verifies bounded frequency/candidate work, QSO-before-wideband
ordering, two-callsign validation, unchanged general candidate quota and the
absence of a competing decoder path.
